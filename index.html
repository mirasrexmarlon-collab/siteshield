Questions

 

Is SiteShield free?

Yes, every feature is free during Early Access. Later, the Free plan stays free (up to 3 sites) and Pro is a one-time payment. Early Supporter keys keep Pro for good.

Which browsers does it work with?

All of them. SiteShield blocks at the Windows level, so Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi and others are all covered. Browsers with a built-in VPN, or set to their own "secure DNS" provider, can go around it; turn those off on managed PCs.

Can my child or a student remove it?

Not from a standard Windows account: stopping, changing or uninstalling SiteShield needs administrator rights, and uninstalling also asks for the SiteShield passcode. Give restricted users a standard account, not an administrator one.

Does it collect my data?

No. There's no account and no cloud. The blocklist, settings and activity log stay on your PC.

What if I forget the passcode?

A Windows administrator can reset it by deleting C:\ProgramData\SiteShield\security_config.json. The passcode then becomes 123456; change it right away in the dashboard.

Why does my browser show a security warning on blocked sites?

A blocked site is answered by SiteShield on your own PC, and the browser notices it isn't the real site. That's expected: the site stays blocked, and the SiteShield passcode prompt appears on your desktop.
